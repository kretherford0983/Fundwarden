PennyWarden for Mac (Apple Silicon)
==================================

Install
  1. Drag PennyWarden onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Open PennyWarden from Applications.

The first time, macOS will say that it could not verify the app
  PennyWarden is free, open-source software and is not registered with Apple,
  so macOS asks you to confirm once:
  1. In the message, click Done (not "Move to Trash").
  2. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  3. Next to "PennyWarden was blocked...", click Open Anyway and confirm.
  After that it opens normally. (On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead
  right-click PennyWarden in Applications and choose Open.)

  If you want to check the download first, compare it with SHA256SUMS.txt
  from the same release:   shasum -a 256 PennyWarden-<version>-macos-arm64.dmg

Use
  A small PennyWarden window appears and your browser opens. Closing that
  window (or Quit) stops PennyWarden. Your data is kept in
  ~/Library/Application Support/PennyWarden - never inside the app - so
  replacing the app with a newer version keeps everything.

This app is for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 11 or later.
